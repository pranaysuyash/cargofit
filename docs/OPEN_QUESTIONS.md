# Cargo Fit — Open Questions & Long-Term Opportunity Map

**Date:** 20 October 2026  

---

## 1. Open Strategic Questions (To Test with Real Operators)
1. **WTP (Willingness to Pay) & Monetization Wedge:** Do mid-market freight forwarders prefer a per-seat monthly license (~$50–80/mo like EasyCargo), a per-shipment export fee, or an API consumption model integrated with their Transport Management System (TMS)?
2. **Spreadsheet Workflow Realities:** How frequently do warehouse planners work from unformatted Excel files vs. standardized CSV/EDI 214 manifests? Should direct `.xlsx` parsing with sheet-selector UI be prioritized next?
3. **Multi-Stop Unload Friction:** Is loading order by stop priority sufficient for dock crews, or do operators require full visual 3D simulation of partial unload states to check forward-axle overload at each intermediate delivery stop?

---

## 2. Adjacent Capabilities Deferred to Future Milestones
- **Multi-Vehicle Splitter:** When a manifest exceeds a single trailer, intelligently partitioning cargo across a minimum fleet (e.g. 1 × 53ft trailer + 1 × 26ft box truck).
- **TMS & ERP Integrations:** Two-way webhooks and REST APIs for CargoWise, McLeod, SAP, and Flex.
- **Vision/Photo Intake:** Smartphone camera dimensional estimation for odd-sized unboxed machinery or return freight.
- **Axle Bridge Formula Calculator:** Optional advanced module allowing operators to enter exact kingpin positions, tractor wheelbase, and axle group ratings for US/Canadian interstate compliance checks.
